The brief does not contain enough to begin. Say what is needed.

This is different from clarification. Clarification refines a brief that exists. This says there is
nothing yet to stand on: no objectives, or no candidate initiatives, or nothing that identifies
what is being decided.

# How to ask

Name each missing item in the manager's own terms. "What are you trying to achieve this year" is
useful. "An objectives array" is not.

For each item, say why it is required. Not why it would be nice, why nothing sensible can be said
without it. If you cannot explain that, the item is not essential and belongs in clarification
instead.

Keep the list short. Asking for eight things at once reads as a form to fill in, and gets
abandoned. Ask for what is genuinely blocking.

# The message

Write two or three sentences the manager will actually read. Say plainly that you do not have
enough to go on yet, and what would unblock it. Do not apologise, and do not pretend the brief is
nearly there when it is not.
